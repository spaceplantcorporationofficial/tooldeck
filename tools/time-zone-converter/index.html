How to use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Pick the date and time, and the time zone it is in.
  2. Add the cities or time zones you want to compare.
  3. Read the matching local time and UTC offset for each zone. Share the URL to send the same comparison to others.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your browser's built-in IANA time zone database, so DST changes for each zone are applied for the exact date you choose.

What is UTC?

Coordinated Universal Time is the global reference time. Time zones are written as offsets from it, for example UTC−5 for New York in winter and UTC−4 in summer.

Why does a converted time show a different date?

When a time crosses midnight in another zone, the date changes too. For example, 9 PM in Los Angeles is already the next morning in Tokyo.

Which time zones are supported?

Every zone your browser knows, which is typically more than 400 IANA zones such as America/New_York, Europe/London and Asia/Kolkata.
