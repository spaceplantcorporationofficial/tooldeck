Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

EMI (equated monthly installment) is the fixed monthly payment that pays off a loan, interest included, by the end of its term. It is calculated as P·r·(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months.

How do extra payments save money?

Any extra amount goes straight to principal, so less interest builds up every month after. Even small extra payments can cut months or years off a loan.

Does this work for car, personal and student loans?

Yes, for any fixed-rate loan with equal monthly payments. Variable-rate loans and loans with fees work differently.

Should I check for prepayment penalties?

Yes. Some lenders charge a fee for paying early. Check your loan agreement before making extra payments.
