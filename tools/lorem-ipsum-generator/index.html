Frequently asked questions

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin placeholder text derived from Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (45 BC). Designers have used it since the 1500s so readers focus on layout, not content.

Why use placeholder text instead of real copy?

It shows how text will flow and fill a layout before final content exists, without distracting reviewers with meaning.

Does it mean anything?

Not really. The words are fragments of Latin with letters added and removed, so it reads like natural text but has no coherent meaning.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. Lorem ipsum is in the public domain.
