How to use the Tip & Bill Split Calculator

  1. Enter the bill total and, optionally, the tax it includes.
  2. Pick a tip percentage with the slider or a quick button.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting, and turn on rounding if you want even amounts.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

In the US, 15–20% is standard for table service, with 20% or more for excellent service. Customs vary widely by country; in many places service is included or tipping is optional.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Etiquette guides traditionally suggest tipping on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the total for simplicity. Turn on "Tip on pre-tax amount" to follow the traditional rule.

How does rounding work?

With rounding on, each share is rounded up to the next whole dollar. The extra goes to the tip, and the real tip percentage is shown.

Is this saved anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated on your device.
