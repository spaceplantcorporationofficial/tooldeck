Frequently asked questions

How many centimeters are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimeters. An inch is defined as 25.4 millimeters.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 20 °C × 9/5 + 32 = 68 °F. To go back, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is about 2.20462 pounds. One pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

What is the difference between MB and MiB?

A megabyte (MB) is 1,000,000 bytes, while a mebibyte (MiB) is 1,048,576 bytes (2^20). Storage makers use MB/GB; operating systems often show MiB/GiB.
