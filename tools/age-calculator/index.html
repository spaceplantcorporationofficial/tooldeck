How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick a date of birth.
  2. Optionally change "Age on this date" to work out age at a past or future date.
  3. Read the exact age, next-birthday countdown and totals in other units.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator counts full years first, then full months, then remaining days, the same way age is counted on official documents. Month lengths and leap years are handled automatically.

What if I was born on February 29?

In non-leap years this calculator treats February 28 as your birthday, for both your age and the countdown. Some countries, such as the UK, legally use March 1 instead, so check local rules for legal deadlines.

Can I calculate age at a specific date?

Yes. Change the "Age on this date" field to any past or future date, for example to check eligibility on a deadline.

Is my birth date stored?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.
