Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 80 is 80 × 15 / 100 = 12.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 out of 120 is 30 / 120 × 100 = 25%.

How is percentage change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. Going from 50 to 65 is (65 − 50) / 50 × 100 = +30%.

What is the difference between percent change and percentage points?

If a rate rises from 4% to 5%, that is a 1 percentage point increase but a 25% relative increase.
